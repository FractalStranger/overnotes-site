Privacy policy

Overnotes · last updated 25 October 2026

Who runs Overnotes

Overnotes is a private app run by Lukas Smida for his band and friends. Contact: fractalstranger@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Your Google account basics when you sign in with Google: your name, email address and profile picture. We use them to identify you and show who wrote a comment.
  • What you create in the app: recordings you upload or record, their titles, folders, comments, replies and likes, and which recordings you have opened (to show new-comment markers).
  • Google Drive access, only if you create a project: permission to create and manage the files Overnotes itself creates in your Drive (the drive.file permission). We store the access token encrypted on the Overnotes server. Overnotes cannot see any other files in your Drive.

Where it is stored

Account data, comments and project structure are stored in a database on the computer that runs Overnotes. Recordings are stored either in the project owner's Google Drive (in an overnotes folder) or on that same computer.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you and the people you share projects with. We do not sell your data, show ads or share it with anyone else. Google processes data as the provider of sign-in and Drive.

Overnotes' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Who can see what

People invited to a project can see its published recordings and their comments, according to their role. Unpublished recordings are visible only to the project's editors.

Removing your data

  • Delete recordings and folders in the app. Files in Google Drive are moved to Drive's trash.
  • Revoke Overnotes' access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To have your account and data deleted, email fractalstranger@gmail.com.
